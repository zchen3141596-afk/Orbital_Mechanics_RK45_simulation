# Earth-Moon-probe RK45 numerical simulation 

Python RK45 numerical simulation of the Earth-Moon and Earth-Moon-probe systems for computational physics and orbital mechanics

The project investigates circular and elliptical motion under Newtonian gravity and evaluates numerical stability through conservation of total mechanical energy. 

This repository accompanies the Bristol Institute for Learning and Teaching (BILT) Student Research Journal paper: 

"Python Numerical Simulation of the Earth-Moon-probe System with RK45 Method" 


##Overview 

This project models: 
- Circular and elliptical Earth-Moon orbits 
- Circular and elliptical Earth-Moon-probe orbits 
- Energy conservation behaviuiour in gravitational systems 

The simulations are implemented in Python using the RK45 numerical integration method through "scipy.integrate.solve_ivp". 

The project aims to demonstrate hyow numerical methods can be applied to systems of ordinary differential equations derived from Newton's and Kepler's laws. 


##Physics Background 

The simulations are based on: 

- Newton's second law and law of gravitation 
- Keplerian orbital motion 
- Conservation of mechanical energy 

The equations of motion are written as systems of first-order ordinary differential equations and solved numerically using RK45 integration. 

For circular orbital motion, the orbital velocity is given by 

\[ 
v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r} 
\] 

For elliptical motion, the orbital velocity is given by 

\[
v = \sqrt{GM\left(\frac{2}{r} - \frac{1}{a}\right)}
\]
